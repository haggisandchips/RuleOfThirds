Pick a composition guide and see straight away how an image lines up against it, all one click away from the toolbar icon. Rule of Thirds draws a configurable overlay over the images on any page, to help you judge composition.

AT A GLANCE
* The toolbar icon shows whether the overlay is on for the current tab, with a warning badge if it can't be applied
* Click the icon again to remove the overlay
* Works with local image files as well as websites

CHOOSE YOUR OVERLAY
* Grid - the classic rule-of-thirds arrangement, or anything up to nine rows and columns
* Phi Grid - a 3x3 grid spaced by the golden ratio (1 : 0.618 : 1), or a ratio of your own
* Fibonacci Spiral - in any of 8 different orientations (starting point / direction)

MAKE IT YOURS
* Colour, opacity and width (1-9 pixels) for the lines
* Circles on the intersections, outlined or filled, with their own colour, opacity and radius
* Show or hide any individual line or circle from a click-to-toggle map, with a live preview of exactly what you'll get
* Quick-pick swatches for common colours, plus a full colour spectrum
* Set a minimum image size, so tiny icons and spacers stay out of the way

CROP TO PART OF THE IMAGE
Overlays can be resized within the boundaries of the image so you can play around with tighter compositions.

SUPPORT
* A Site Compatibility page lists the sites the extension is confirmed to work on - if it doesn't work somewhere, raise it on GitHub and I'll try to fix it
